# Trovan Marketing Claims Register

Date: 2026-08-25

Purpose: prevent the website, proposals, demos, outbound messages, social posts, and sales calls from promising more than Trovan can prove. A claim may be used publicly only when its status is `APPROVED` and the evidence still matches the deployed release.

## Claim rules

- `APPROVED`: directly supported by current product or commercial evidence.
- `CONDITIONAL`: usable only with the stated qualifier and audience/context.
- `PROHIBITED`: do not publish or say without new evidence and approval.
- Product screenshots and demos must be from the current release and use synthetic or expressly permitted data.
- Estimates and scenarios must be labeled; they are not customer results.
- Customer names, logos, quotes, metrics, and case studies require written publicity permission.
- Security, privacy, legal, tax, accessibility, certification, and uptime language must match approved evidence and contracts.

## Approved core message draft

Trovan helps delivery and distribution teams plan routes, dispatch the day, guide drivers, share customer status, and keep proof attached to the route record. The assisted pilot is manually approved, operator-supported, and designed for teams that want to test the workflow with a representative route day before a broader rollout.

## Prohibited shorthand

Do not use these phrases without new evidence:

- guaranteed savings
- perfect or optimal routes
- eliminates late deliveries
- real-time everywhere
- enterprise-grade security
- bank-grade encryption
- fully compliant
- SOC 2 ready or certified
- zero downtime / 99.9% uptime
- trusted by leading fleets
- proven ROI
- safest route
- AI-powered, unless the specific user-facing feature and provider behavior are accurately described

## Evidence record required for new quantitative claims

Before approval, record:

- exact claim and every implied meaning
- audience, page/campaign, and date range
- dataset/source and customer permission
- baseline, comparison, methodology, sample size, exclusions, and limitations
- exact product release and configuration
- reviewer and approval date
- expiration/recheck date
- required disclosure next to the claim
